# Reader first

## When it applies

You are writing code, a document, a commit message, or a report that someone else will read later.

## The rule

Write for the next reader, who does not have your context. Lead with what they need, use the names they will search for, and leave out the path you took to get there.

## What it changes

- Name a variable after how it was computed → name it after what it means to the caller.
- Open a report with the steps you ran → open it with the result and what the reader has to do.

## Not this

Explaining every line. A reader-first text is shorter, not longer, because it drops what the reader does not need.
