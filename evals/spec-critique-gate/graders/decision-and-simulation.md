Judge the section headed "## Critique". The Critique section contains (1) a decision record that names at least two viable options for where or how the logging is added and says why the chosen one won, or, if it declares a single viable option, gives a reason the others are invalid; and (2) two or three simulated implementation tasks, as a list or a table, each naming real paths under src/ or test/ from the workspace (for example src/server.mjs or test/server.test.mjs) and stating whether the executor can proceed without guessing or naming the gap. The critique does not add features beyond request logging.
