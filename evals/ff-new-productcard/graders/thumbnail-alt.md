The thumbnail img has an alt attribute, and one of these holds: (a) the alt text carries the product name or another description specific to the product, or (b) the alt is intentionally empty and the answer says why (for example, the product name is rendered as text right next to the image, so a text alternative would be read twice). A missing alt, a generic alt such as "image" or "thumbnail", or an empty alt with no stated reason fails.
