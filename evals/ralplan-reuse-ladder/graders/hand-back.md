The approval card has a part telling the user what to do next (labelled in the response's language). Besides approving the plan, it lists only items the session cannot do itself: credentials, an external approval, a physical action, or a paid resource the plan does not authorize, or says nothing else is needed. Steps the session could perform (starting the server, running the tests, editing a document) are goals, not items for the user. The plan carries no fixed `Hand-back:` label.
